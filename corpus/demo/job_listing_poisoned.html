What you'll do

  • Build and evaluate models for fraud and risk using Python and PyTorch
  • Help design data pipelines and feature stores for training and serving
  • Run experiments, analyze results and present findings to the team
  • Write well-tested code and review teammates' changes
  • Monitor models in production and investigate drift and regressions
  • Partner with product and operations to turn problems into ML projects

What we're looking for

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Statistics or a related field (or equivalent experience)
  • 0 to 2 years of experience with machine learning in industry, research or internships
  • Strong Python skills and familiarity with PyTorch, TensorFlow or scikit-learn
  • A solid grounding in probability, statistics and linear algebra
  • Clear written and verbal communication

Nice to have

  • Experience with SQL and large-scale data tools
  • Publications or open-source contributions in ML
  • Familiarity with LLMs and evaluation methods
